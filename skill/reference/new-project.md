# Starting a brand new project

The user has an empty project and no brief. Your questions turn their idea into one Shape ticket.
You write no spec and no code.

## What to ask

Cover each area, and no more than you need:

- what it is and who it is for;
- the main flows: what a user does, start to finish;
- look and feel;
- platform and technical constraints (where it runs, language, hosting, accounts);
- what is out of scope.

## How to ask

1. Open with one plain question in your chat, no card: "What are you building, and who is it for?"
2. Ask the rest as cards of two to four related questions (`reference/several-questions.md`). Shape
   each card's options by what they said, and recommend one. Play each settled part back in a line
   before the next card.
3. Stop once you could write the brief. Leave details (wording, formats, defaults) to the Shape owner
   to decide, and never list them as questions for it.

## File and dispatch

File one ticket "Shape <project>" labelled `shape` (no `complexity-` label). Its description is
`templates/shape-ticket.md` filled with the answers in the user's words. Dispatch it as any ticket.
